## Introduction

Concord Voice is a privacy-first place to talk with your friends, your community, or your team. It gives you voice calls, video, screen sharing, and chat. Your device encrypts each message before it leaves, so our servers hold only scrambled data that we cannot read. You can use our hosted service, or you can run the same software on your own hardware.

The Bazaar will be the place to find themes, sound packs, bots, and other add-ons for Concord Voice.
Every add-on will be free to install, and creators can list a donation link.

Nothing ships from here yet. The groundwork is planned for Concord Voice v1.1.0.

For guides and background, see the [The-Bazaar wiki](https://github.com/Concord-Voice/The-Bazaar/wiki).

## Technical overview

The Bazaar will be the community marketplace for Concord Voice: themes, sound packs, bots and other add-ons, all free to install, with optional donation links for their creators.

**Status: placeholder.** Nothing ships from here yet. The marketplace groundwork is targeted for **Concord Voice v1.1.0**.

### What is planned

- **A curated manifest registry.** Each add-on is described by a reviewed manifest in this repository. Add-on files never live in Git.
- **Signed downloads.** Approved add-ons are served from a signed edge mirror, and the client checks the signature of the catalog before installing anything.
- **Free for everyone.** Installing is free. Creators can list a donation link.
- **Reviewed contributions.** Submissions run through automated checks and a maintainer review before they reach the catalog.
